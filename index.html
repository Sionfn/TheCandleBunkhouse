Our Story

From Hobby
to Heart

"Wherever life plants you, bloom with grace."

What started as a deep love of fragrance blossomed into something truly beautiful. Raquel Thompson White began her candle-making journey as a simple hobby — a passion for scents and the therapeutic joy of creating something with her hands.

Inspired by her eldest son's unwavering belief in her abilities, she faced every challenge head-on: finding the right wick, perfecting fragrance ratios, eliminating every flaw. Through trial, error, and determination, The Candle Bunkhouse, LLC was born.

Today, Raquel and her husband John — both southpaws and proud Arizonans — pour every candle by hand. They travel, connect with customers at craft shows, and keep pouring love into every tin and jar that leaves their studio.
