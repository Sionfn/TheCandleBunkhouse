Meet Raquel

From Hobby
to Heart

"Wherever life plants you,
bloom with grace."

What started as a deep love of fragrance blossomed into something truly beautiful. Raquel Thompson White began her candle-making journey as a simple hobby — a passion for scents and the therapeutic joy of creating with her hands.

Inspired by her eldest son's unwavering belief in her abilities, she tackled every challenge head-on: finding the right wick, perfecting fragrance ratios, eliminating every flaw. Through trial, error, and pure determination, The Candle Bunkhouse, LLC was born.

Today, Raquel and her husband John — both proud left-handed Arizonans — pour every candle by hand in Saint David. They travel, connect with customers at craft shows across the Southwest, and pour love into every single tin that leaves their studio.

Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Your satisfaction is our top priority. We stand behind the quality of every candle we make. Please review our return and refund policy below.

Our Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we want to hear from you. We will do our best to make it right.

Returns

Due to the handcrafted and consumable nature of our candles, we generally do not accept returns on items that have been burned or used. However, we accept returns of unused, unopened items within 14 days of delivery. Items must be in their original condition and packaging.

To Initiate a Return

  • Email us at hello@thecandlebunkhouse.com within 14 days of receiving your order
  • Include your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason for return
  • We will provide a return shipping address and instructions
  • Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to our error

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Please note that original shipping charges are non-refundable.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please email us at hello@thecandlebunkhouse.com within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund at no cost to you — no return required for damaged items.

Wrong Item Received

If you receive the wrong item, contact us immediately at hello@thecandlebunkhouse.com. We will ship the correct item and cover all associated costs.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Candles that have been burned or used
  • Items marked as final sale
  • Gift cards
